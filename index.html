OUR DELIVERY SERVICE AREAS

Gifting services in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad and Rawalpindi.

Giftivity accepts gift requests from customers in Pakistan and abroad for delivery in four cities: Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad and Rawalpindi. We coordinate with local suppliers, then contact you to confirm product availability, final pricing and delivery details. Please place orders at least 24 hours before the requested delivery date.

Lahore: gift delivery coordination, flower bouquets, customised gifts, cakes and gift baskets.

Karachi: gift delivery coordination, flower bouquets, customised gifts, cakes and gift baskets.

Islamabad: gift delivery coordination, flower bouquets, customised gifts, cakes and gift baskets.

Rawalpindi: gift delivery coordination, flower bouquets, customised gifts, cakes and gift baskets.

Giftivity is an independent gift-ordering service. Product photographs and prices are indicative; exact items, supplier availability and fulfilment are confirmed before an order is accepted.

OUR BOOKING POLICY

24-hour advance booking
is required for every order.

Please place your order at least 24 hours before delivery. Standard delivery is 10:00 AM–5:00 PM. A selected two-hour delivery window between 10:00 AM and 10:00 PM adds PKR 300. Standard delivery costs PKR 300 per order. Advance payment is required before an order is processed. Customers may pay by cash collection or bank deposit / bank transfer.
