OUR BOOKING POLICY

24-hour advance booking
is required for every order.

Please place your request at least 24 hours before the delivery date. Standard delivery is scheduled between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM. You may select a two-hour delivery window between 10:00 AM and 10:00 PM for an additional PKR 300; selected windows are subject to confirmation. We first check supplier availability, delivery coverage and the final price, then contact you for confirmation. Special/custom cakes and personalised products may need additional notice.
