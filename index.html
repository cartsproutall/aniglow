A LITTLE PLANNING, A LOVELY SURPRISE

Book at least 24 hours in advance.

Please place every order at least 24 hours before your preferred delivery date. Standard delivery between 10:00 AM and 5:00 PM is free in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad and Rawalpindi. If available, you may request a two-hour delivery window between 10:00 AM and 10:00 PM for an additional PKR 300. Advance payment is required before an order is processed.
